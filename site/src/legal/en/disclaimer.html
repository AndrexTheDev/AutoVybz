Demo and claims

The audio demo on this website is a simulation of a typical small speaker in a typical room and of a typical correction. It is level-matched but illustrative only. Actual results depend on your speaker, room, placement, phone and microphone. Statements such as "±10 dB room-related bass deviation" describe typical acoustic effects, not a guaranteed measurement of your setup.

Speaker Roast

The Speaker Roast is entertainment. Scores and diagnoses are rough rules of thumb based on typical room acoustics and do not replace a measurement.

No professional advice, not a medical device

AutoVybz is a hobby and consumer tool. It does not replace professional acoustic measurement or consulting and is not a medical device or hearing aid. It does not measure or protect your hearing.

Hearing and equipment

Loud music can permanently damage your hearing; keep volumes moderate. Equalisation can raise the load on speakers and amplifiers. AutoVybz limits bass boosts and uses pre-gain and a limiter, but you remain responsible for using your equipment within its limits.

Microphone accuracy

Built-in phone microphones differ from device to device. The included compensation profiles are estimates of typical microphone behaviour, not laboratory measurements of individual devices.
