3. Local storage (language choice)

If you actively choose a language, this choice is stored in your browser's localStorage under the key avz-lang so the site opens in your language next time. This value never leaves your device. It is strictly necessary for the function you requested (Art. 5(3) ePrivacy Directive and its national implementations, e.g. § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). You can delete it at any time via your browser settings. If you don't choose a language, the site follows your browser's language setting without storing anything.

4. Audio demo and Speaker Roast

The demo plays a short audio file that is part of this website. It is encoded and played locally by your browser with the Web Audio API; nothing is uploaded and no external media server is contacted. Your microphone is not used. The file is only downloaded once you press play (the player starts with preload="none"). Your Speaker Roast answers and the generated share card are processed only in your browser and are not transmitted to us.

5. Share buttons

The share buttons are plain links. No data is sent to any social network when you load this page. Only when you click a button are you taken to the respective platform (e.g. WhatsApp, X, Reddit), whose privacy policy then applies.

6. Star count

The GitHub star count shown on the page is fetched when the website is built, not by your browser. Your browser makes no request to the GitHub API.

7. Contact by e-mail

If you e-mail us, we process your address and message solely to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR) and delete them once they are no longer needed, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

8. Crypto payments and donations

This website only displays crypto addresses and QR codes; it has no checkout or wallet and receives no payment data from your browser. Transactions are recorded publicly on the respective blockchain by their nature. We do not try to link wallet addresses to persons. If you contact us about a payment, we process the details you send (for example a TXID) only to handle that request.

10. The AutoVybz app

The app has its own privacy policy: AutoVybz app privacy policy. In short: the measurement audio is analysed only on your phone and never uploaded. If you activate Pro with a TXID, the app stores only a SHA-256 hash of that TXID, plus the plan and expiry, on the device.
