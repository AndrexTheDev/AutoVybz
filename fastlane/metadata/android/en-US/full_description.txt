<b>One tap. 10 seconds. Your speaker, tuned to your room.</b>

AutoVybz automatically calibrates your external loudspeaker (Bluetooth, AUX, USB, HDMI) to your room using your phone's microphone, then applies the correction system-wide.

<b>How it works</b>
<ul>
<li>Exactly 10 s test: room noise, pink noise and a logarithmic sine sweep from 20 Hz to 20 kHz</li>
<li>Raw microphone input (UNPROCESSED, fallback VOICE_RECOGNITION) via a native Oboe/C++ engine</li>
<li>Automatic latency detection – works with Bluetooth speakers</li>
<li>Microphone compensation profiles by phone model; USB measurement mics with calibration files (Pro)</li>
<li>Dynamic smoothing (strong in the bass, 1/3 octave in mids and highs)</li>
<li>Up to 10 parametric filters fitted to your target, without boosting bass your speaker can't play</li>
<li>Automatic pre-gain and limiter against clipping</li>
<li>Precise mode: 3 positions, power-averaged – ignores dips that exist only at one spot (AutoVybz Pro / trial; Free uses 1 position)</li>
<li>Outdoor / party mode: max +3 dB boost to keep the loudness, ignores ground reflections and noisy ranges (wind, traffic, birds), detects wind and Bluetooth dropouts, limiter always on, full screen brightness in sunlight</li>
</ul>

<b>Targets</b>: Smart (gentle bass boost), Neutral/Flat, Bass Boost, Harman/Loudness, Custom.

<b>Pro area</b>: full Equalizer314 engine – 10-band parametric EQ with graph, multiband compressor, limiter, presets, AutoEQ.

<b>Export &amp; share</b>: AutoVybz Pro exports Equalizer APO / Peace text, parametric EQ JSON and CSV for REW. Free keeps internal presets and the share card with your real measurement.

<b>Free &amp; Pro</b>: Free includes the permanent banner, one saved profile and quick 1-position calibration. A 7-day Pro trial starts after the first result. Pro is US$1.99/month, US$19.99/year or US$99 lifetime in the Alpha, payable only with SOL, BTC or ETH and without auto-renewal.

Available in English, German and Spanish.

Headphones are intentionally excluded – AutoVybz is made for loudspeakers.

Open source (GPL-3.0), no account, no tracking and no ad SDK. Based on Equalizer314 by bearinmindcat.
