## Based on Equalizer314

AutoVybz is a fork of **Equalizer314** by **bearinmindcat**
(https://github.com/bearinmindcat/Equalizer314), imported at upstream commit `90dd51a`
(v0.0.22-beta) and licensed under the GNU GPL v3.0. The complete EQ engine (DynamicsProcessing,
10-band parametric EQ, multiband compressor, limiter, AutoEQ, presets, TV mode, virtual surround)
is upstream work. Many thanks to bearinmindcat for making it open source.

Because Equalizer314 is GPL-3.0, AutoVybz as a whole is GPL-3.0 as well. (The original AutoVybz
repository started with an MIT license file; that does not apply to this combined work.)

Code layout:

- `app/src/main/java/com/bearinmind/equalizer314/` — upstream Equalizer314 (minimal AutoVybz hooks,
  marked with `AutoVybz` comments)
- `app/src/main/java/com/andrexthedev/autovybz/` — AutoVybz (calibration wizard, measurement DSP,
  mic profiles, exports, Compose UI)
- `app/src/main/cpp/autovybz/` — AutoVybz native measurement engine

## Data notes

- `app/src/main/assets/autovybz/mic_profiles.json` (generated by `tools/micdb/generate_mic_profiles.py`)
  contains **estimated** archetype curves of typical MEMS smartphone microphones, not lab measurements
  of the listed devices.
- The "Harman / Loudness" target is an analytic approximation (bass shelf + tilt) of the published
  research on preferred in-room loudspeaker response; it does not reproduce proprietary data.
